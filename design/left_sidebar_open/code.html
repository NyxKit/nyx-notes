Project Obsidian Protocol

Architecture In Progress Draft v.02

In the quiet depth of the workspace, we find the true meaning of clarity. The design system we are building is not merely a collection of buttons and sliders, but a sanctuary for focus. We call it The Silent Atelier.

We avoid the noise of 1px borders and standard grid systems. Instead, we embrace the concept of tonal layering. Each component should feel as though it is emerging from the dark obsidian of the screen, rather than sitting on top of it.

"The interface should be felt, but rarely seen. It is the vessel, not the wine."

The core philosophy remains simple: reduce ocular strain by using low-saturation purples and layered monochromatic surfaces. Ensure that the cursor—the bridge between mind and machine—always has a soft glow of #cbc2e4 to guide the user's focus back to their thoughts.
